Privacy Policy for MetaMer

Last updated: September 13, 2026

MetaMer ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a voice-activated AI assistant developed by Biswanath Nanda. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account or sign in, we (via our authentication provider, Supabase) collect your email address and, if you use Sign in with Google, basic profile information (name, email, profile photo) provided by Google.

Content you provide. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • Text messages and voice recordings you send to the assistant
  • Images you share for image analysis
  • Resumes and job descriptions you upload to the ATS Resume Center

This content is processed only to generate a response for you in the moment (e.g., transcribing your voice, analyzing an image, or matching a resume to a job description). We do not retain or store this content after it has been processed — see "Data Retention" below.

Usage and diagnostic data. We may collect basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash/error logs to help us maintain and improve the App.

Subscription and billing information. If you subscribe to Pro or Premium plans, billing is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles all payment processing.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core assistant functionality: conversational responses, voice transcription, and voice replies
  • To analyze images and documents you submit (e.g., resume/job description matching in the ATS Resume Center)
  • To authenticate your account and sync your data across sessions
  • To process subscriptions and manage your plan tier (Free, Pro, Premium)
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App

3. Third-Party Services

MetaMer relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Supabase — authentication and account data storage
  • Groq — cloud-based AI inference used to generate conversational responses when local processing is unavailable or not selected
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchase processing
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method

Voice transcription and speech processing may be performed locally on your device or via cloud inference depending on your configuration. Text and voice content sent to cloud-based inference providers (such as Groq) is transmitted to generate a response and is subject to that provider's own data handling practices.

Each third-party service processes data according to its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective policies.

4. Data Retention

Account information (such as your email address and login credentials) is retained for as long as your account remains active, so that you can sign in and access the App.

Content data is not retained. We do not store your messages, voice recordings, transcriptions, images, resumes, or job descriptions after they have been processed and a response has been returned to you. This content is used transiently to generate your result and is then discarded.

You may request deletion of your account and its login information at any time (see "Your Rights" below).

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

MetaMer is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may access, update, or delete your account information at any time from within the App.
  • You may request full deletion of your account and its login information by contacting us at nanda.biswanath@gmail.com. Since we do not retain your conversation, voice, image, or document content, there is no additional content data to delete beyond your account record.
  • Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
